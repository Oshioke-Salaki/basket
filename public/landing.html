A price check before you pay. At the shelf, the stall or abroad.

Inflation measured on your own basket. Not a national average of things you never buy.

Basket follows every item you buy more than once and weights its price change by what you spend on it. That gives one number: how much dearer your own shopping has become.

  • In your home currency and in US dollars
  • Trips abroad priced against home, at the rate of each day
  • The items pushing it up, ranked by their weight
  • Your stores ranked on the items you share
  • Your groceries counted in weeks of a Sui wallet’s USDC and SUI, read through Tatum

Memory that stays yours

Every receipt and every lesson is encrypted with Seal and stored on Walrus Memory on Sui mainnet. Basket keeps no database of its own: each visit recalls your basket from Walrus.

A basket key opens it on any phone. Nobody else can read it without that key, and there is no reset link: lose the key and the basket is gone.

  • No account, no email address
  • Receipts and lessons kept in two Walrus namespaces
  • Each write stamped with a Sui checkpoint, read through Tatum
  • The same receipt scanned twice is recognised
